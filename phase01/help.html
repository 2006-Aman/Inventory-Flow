How is the Reorder Point (ROP) calculated for inventory items?

Reorder Point is calculated using: ROP = (Average Daily Sales Ã— Lead Time Days) + Safety Stock. When an item's current stock falls below this threshold, it automatically triggers a Reorder Alert badge.

How does the app handle offline data when json-server is closed?

The system includes a dual storage fallback architecture. If json-server is stopped, the application automatically switches to LocalStorage data persistence, allowing you to seamlessly create products, record sales, and view reports without interruption.

How do automatic Category SKU codes work?

On the Add Product page, selecting or typing a Category (e.g. Electronics, Groceries, Frozen Foods) automatically generates a category-prefixed SKU (such as ELE-0001 or GROC-0002).

How do I export my sales and business reports?

Open either the Sales History or Reports page and click the "Export CSV" button located at the top right. A downloadable CSV file containing your formatted data will be generated instantly.

How does the Moving Average Forecast Engine work?

The forecast engine analyzes your recent 7-day or 14-day sales velocity to calculate average daily sales trends and project expected stock depletion dates, helping you restock before running out.
